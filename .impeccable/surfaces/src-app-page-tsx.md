Scope: whole marketing site (Home sets the world; every page inherits). Visitor mode: Persuade (Home, Products, Partnership, Management); Operate for Contact and Training registration; Read for policy pages.

Audience and job: Lagos homeowners and small businesses on phones who want a trustworthy installer they can talk to; commercial, industrial and EPC buyers who judge capability from specifics. Action: Get a Quote (single contact funnel) or WhatsApp. Proof: real specs, process, terms; credited photography that is never presented as Reohob's own projects.

User feedback driving this redesign (2026-11-20). Also: only facts that appear on reohobenergy.com are published (no placeholders or sample content); Projects is hidden until real projects exist.
Earlier feedback: the previous "solar chart" world felt cold, its type too heavy, and it had no imagery. Keep the technical credibility. Balance homeowners and engineers.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Reohob speaks the language of Lagos's 1960s–70s tropical modernism: breeze-block sun screens, louvres, warm board-marked concrete and deep tropical green. Architecture built to manage the sun, for a company that manages the sun. It refuses the category default (a stock rooftop hero over a blue-green gradient with an icon-card grid) and its own previous cold, chart-paper world.

OWN-WORLD: White reading ground. Warm concrete bands (#ECE9E3). Deep tropical green (#1C3B2E) as the single solid conversion band and footer. Green-black ink. Marigold sun (#F5A300) reserved for the main action and active state. Laterite clay (#B4532F) for small accents. Display type is Jost, a geometric modernist sans set at 500–600, never black; body is Public Sans. Corners are square. Professional photography (Pexels: arrays, homes, EV canopy, crews in full PPE with faces turned away, Lagos at night) in clean crops, with no frame, pattern or on-page credit (client decision 2026-11-20: the breeze-block screen was removed, and credits are recorded in src/content/photos.ts only).

STORY: The visitor sees a real Nigerian installer on a rooftop array and Reohob's promise in one calm line. They see today's sun over Lagos and the day/night mechanism in a compact strip, then pick their service. Next come process, terms and people, each page opening on one decisive photograph. They act: Get a Quote or WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: left 55%: H1 "Power that stays on, day and night." in Jost 500 at about 4.75rem in green-black, a one-line sub, Get a Quote (marigold) and WhatsApp (outline). Right 45%: a tall photograph of rows of solar panels running towards a low sun, in a clean crop. Under the buttons sits a one-line live Lagos-time note. Below: a warm concrete figures strip, then the services index, then a concrete band holding the live sun-path chart. Mobile: headline, buttons, then the photo at 4:5.

FORM: Grounded candidate #4 of 7 (Lagos tropical modernism). Seed key 48be9d3b. Signature: Lagos-modernist warmth carried by concrete bands, deep tropical green and medium-weight Jost, plus the live sun chart kept small and honest. The breeze-block motif was removed at the client's request. Raises: one decisive photograph per page (Polish poster). Solid green fill reserved for the one conversion band per page (manga). Bands and rules, not boxed cards (cracktro). Marigold marks the main action and the active state only (chromatophore). Every figure keeps its trend; the sun strip stays (six-pack). The hero copy follows Lagos time: battery message after sunset (sleeping city).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
