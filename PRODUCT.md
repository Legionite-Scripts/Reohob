## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router, TypeScript) + Tailwind CSS, specified in the brief. Hosting: Vercel (confirmed). Form email: SMTP through the reohobenergy.com mailbox host (confirmed). Payments: Paystack, verified server-side.

## Users

Two audiences, confirmed in the brief:

1. **Lagos homeowners and small businesses** who live with unreliable grid power and diesel generators, and want a solar, inverter and battery system sized for their home or shop. They usually arrive on a mid-range Android phone, often on a slow or metered connection, and will want to talk to a person (WhatsApp or a phone call) before paying.
2. **Commercial, industrial and EPC clients**: facility managers, procurement teams and engineers who need a contractor that can survey, design, procure, install and maintain larger plants. They also cover owner's-engineer and project-management work. They judge credibility from specifics: specs, scope, process, people, and policies.

Secondary: people who want to train as solar installers (Training), and organisations that want to fund or partner on rural energy (Partnership).

## Product Purpose

The website for Reohob Limited, a Lagos-based renewable energy and engineering company. It must win enquiries from both audiences and route them to one contact funnel. It must sell and take payment for the engineering training programme. It must state warranty, fee and financing terms once and consistently. Success = qualified enquiries (with the service and load details the team needs) and paid training registrations.

## Positioning

A Lagos engineering contractor that designs and installs complete solar, inverter and battery systems from 1 kVA homes to 100 kVA commercial sites. It supplies EV chargers and airport-grade security screening equipment, and runs its own installer training academy. It also offers financing, with spread payments over up to 12 months.

## Operating Context

- Customers decide over WhatsApp and phone; forms must capture load details (property type, rooms, appliances) so the team can size a system.
- Site survey, then design, installation, then support/maintenance is the real delivery sequence (from the old site's service list and policies).
- Installations in Lagos happen 24–48 hours after payment is confirmed; outside Lagos by agreed date.
- Training is sold online with Paystack; online and on-site packages.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Products: solar panels 250–445 W (60- to 72-cell bifacial mono PERC double-glass), inverters 1–100 kVA, batteries (lead-acid, lithium-ion, carbon-lead; 100–210 Ah, mostly 12 V).
- Services: supply and installation, site survey and audit report, EPC support, maintenance/servicing/metering of existing plants, preliminary BOQ, hire an engineering team. New service lines named in the brief: Owner's Engineer, OEM, Project Management (copy is draft).
- Security screening equipment: X-ray baggage scanners, walk-through metal detectors, explosive trace detectors.
- Financing: 1–12 months, 3% monthly flat rate, subject to verification (bank statements; CAC documents for businesses).
- **Undecided facts (placeholders, never guessed):** years in business, warranty lengths per product, call-out fee, working hours, WhatsApp number, trust numbers, partner list currency (Rapiscan), whether EV / security / training lines are all active, whether reohob.com can be recovered, whether a shop or chatbot is wanted.

## Brand Commitments

## Product Principles

1. One source of truth: every company fact, fee, rate and warranty comes from config, so contradictions cannot return.
2. Proof over adjectives: show specs, process, scope and terms; never claim what can't be shown.
3. Talk to a person fast: WhatsApp, phone and the single contact form are always one tap away.
4. Built for the phone in a Lagos customer's hand: light pages, fast on slow connections, readable in sunlight.
5. Credible to engineers: precise units, honest scope, plain terms.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard navigation, visible focus, AA contrast, reduced-motion support, labelled forms. No horizontal scroll at 390, 768, 1440 px. Must stay usable on mid-range Android and slow 3G/4G.
